# SaaS Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behavior within a SaaS subscription business to understand product engagement, customer retention, subscription performance, revenue patterns and factors associated with customer churn.

The analysis transforms customer-level usage and subscription data into actionable business insights that can support customer retention, product adoption and revenue growth.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand customer behavior and identify patterns that can help a SaaS business:

- Improve customer engagement
- Reduce customer churn
- Strengthen customer retention
- Optimize subscription plans
- Improve product adoption
- Identify revenue opportunities

---

## Business Problem

A SaaS company needs to understand why customers remain active or leave the platform.

The analysis focuses on key business questions such as:

- Which customers are most engaged with the product?
- Which subscription plans have higher churn?
- How does product usage relate to customer retention?
- Does customer tenure influence churn?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- Is customer support activity associated with churn?
- How can the business improve customer retention?

---

## Analysis Areas

### 1. Customer & Data Exploration

The dataset was explored to understand:

- Customer records
- Dataset structure
- Variable types
- Numerical summaries
- Customer behavior attributes

---

### 2. Customer Engagement Analysis

Customer engagement was analyzed using factors such as:

- Monthly usage hours
- Subscription type
- Customer tenure
- Product usage behavior

The objective was to identify customer groups showing stronger product engagement.

---

### 3. Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

The analysis examines:

- Churn across subscription plans
- Churn patterns based on customer tenure
- Product usage vs churn
- Customer segments with higher churn risk

This helps identify potential retention challenges and customer groups that may require intervention.

---

### 4. Revenue Analysis

Revenue patterns were analyzed across:

- Subscription plans
- Customer segments
- Engagement levels

The analysis helps identify segments that contribute more strongly to revenue and understand the relationship between customer engagement and revenue.

---

### 5. Customer Support Analysis

Customer support behavior was analyzed to understand relationships between:

- Support tickets and churn
- Support tickets and subscription plans
- Support activity and customer engagement

This provides an additional perspective on customer experience and retention.

---

## Key Business Questions

The project aims to answer:

1. Which subscription plans retain customers most effectively?
2. Which customer segments have higher churn risk?
3. How does product usage relate to churn?
4. Does tenure influence customer retention?
5. Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
6. Is support activity related to customer churn?
7. What actions could improve customer retention and product adoption?

---

The visualizations are designed to communicate business findings clearly and support decision-making.

---

## Key Business Insights

The analysis identifies important patterns related to:

- Customer engagement
- Subscription performance
- Customer churn
- Customer retention
- Revenue contribution
- Support activity

These insights help the business understand which customer groups require attention and where retention opportunities may exist.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, potential business actions include:

- Develop targeted retention strategies for high-risk customers.
- Increase engagement among low-usage customers.
- Identify customers showing early signs of churn and introduce proactive interventions.
- Review subscription plans based on customer behavior and retention performance.
- Improve product adoption through targeted onboarding and engagement campaigns.
- Monitor support activity as part of the broader customer experience strategy.
